    How to play Partners

    Goal: you play in a team of two. Get all 8 of your team's parcels from the depots, around the road and into your finish lanes before the other team does.

    The table

    • 4 players, 2 teams. Partners sit opposite each other: Team 1 = Red + Yellow, Team 2 = Green + Blue. The number on each parcel's label shows its team.
    • Each player has 4 parcels in their depot.
    • Parcels come out onto your coloured start square and travel clockwise around the road.
    • After a full lap they turn off at the start square into your finish lane (the 4 coloured squares pointing to the middle).

    A round

    1. Deal. Everyone gets cards: 6 in the first round, then 5, 4, 3 and 2. After that the deck is shuffled and it starts again at 6.
    2. Swap with your partner. You each give your partner 1 card in secret. A card you got this way is marked NEW.
    3. Take turns. Going clockwise, each player plays one card and makes that move.
    4. You must play if you can. If none of your cards can be used, you throw away your whole hand and sit out the rest of the round.
    5. The round ends when all cards are used. The next player becomes dealer.

    Other cardsMove that many squares forward (2, 3, 5, 6, 9, 10).
    ★
    JokerPlays as any card you choose.

    There are two decks with 4 jokers, 108 cards in all.

    Rules on the road

    • Knock-out: land exactly on another parcel and it goes back to its depot. This works on any parcel, including your partner's or your own.
    • Protected start square: a parcel that has just come out (drawn with a ring) can't be knocked out, swapped or passed by anyone. It blocks the road until it moves on.
    • Finish lane: you need the exact count to move in. You can't jump over your own parcels in the lane, and you can't go into it backwards.
    • All your parcels home? Keep playing your cards, but now you move your partner's parcels.

    Tip: play a 4 on a parcel that has just come out. It moves 4 squares back, to just behind its start square, and is then only 5 steps from the finish lane.

    Tip: a parcel waiting on its start square blocks the other team, but it also blocks your partner and your own parcels coming home.

    Who wins

    The first team with all 8 parcels in their finish lanes wins.

    Playing on this screen

    1. Click a card in your hand. Greyed-out cards can't be played right now.
    2. Click a parcel with a blue ring.
    3. If there's a choice, click a yellow square to pick where it goes.
    4. Check the preview, then press Confirm (Enter), or Undo (Backspace) to choose again.

    With hidden hands and several human players, a blue screen covers the table between turns. Pass the laptop to the player it names. M turns the sound on or off.
