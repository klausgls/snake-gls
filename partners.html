    How to play Partners

    Goal: you play in a team of two. Get all 8 of your team's parcels out of the depots, once around the road and into your finish lanes before the other team does.

    The table

    • 4 players, 2 teams. Partners sit opposite each other: Team 1 = Red + Green, Team 2 = Yellow + Blue. The number on each parcel's label shows its team.
    • Each player has 4 parcels in their depot.
    • Parcels come out onto your coloured start square and travel clockwise around the road.
    • After a full lap they turn off at the start square into your finish lane (the 4 coloured squares pointing to the middle).

    A round

    1. Deal. Everyone gets 4 cards. The dealer shuffles once and deals 3 times in a row, and then the next player becomes dealer.
    2. Swap with your partner. You each give your partner 1 card in secret. A card you got this way is marked NEW.
    3. Take turns. Starting with the player after the dealer and going clockwise, each player plays one card and makes that move.
    4. You must play if you can. If none of your cards can be used, you put down your whole hand and wait for the next deal.

    The cards

    Partners has its own deck of 52 cards: 13 kinds, 4 of each.

    ♥
    HeartBring a parcel out of your depot onto your start square.
    ♥8
    Heart / 8Bring a parcel out, or move 8 forward.
    ♥13
    Heart / 13Bring a parcel out, or move 13 forward.
    1/14
    1 / 14Move 1 or 14 forward.
    −4
    Minus 4Move 4 backwards. Not for parcels in the finish lane.
    7
    SevenSplit 7 steps forward over as many parcels as you like. Each parcel moves once, and all 7 must be used.
    ⇄
    SwapSwap any two parcels on the road, even if you have none out yourself.
    2–12
    Number cardsMove that many forward: 2, 3, 5, 6, 9, 10 or 12.

    Only heart cards bring parcels out. If none of your parcels are out and you have no heart, you can't move, but you can still play a Swap.

    Rules on the road

    • Knock-out: land on a parcel of another colour and it goes back to its depot. This includes your partner's parcels.
    • Pairs are safe: two parcels of the same colour on one square (drawn with a ring) protect each other. Land on them and your parcel goes home. Your own parcels may share a square.
    • Start square block: while any of your parcels stand on your own start square, no other colour can pass or land there, not even your partner. Parcels there are ringed and can't be swapped.
    • Finish lane: fill it from the inside. You can't jump over your own parcels there. If your card is too high, the parcel bounces back by the extra steps. Parcels in the lane are safe.
    • All your parcels home? Keep playing your cards, but now you move your partner's parcels. If a 7 gets your last parcel home, the rest of the steps go to your partner.

    Tip: play −4 on a parcel on or just after its start square. It goes 4 squares back and is suddenly almost home.

    Tip: give your partner what they need. If they have no parcels out, a heart is worth more than any number.

    Who wins

    The first team with all 8 parcels in their finish lanes wins.

    Playing on this screen

    1. Click a card in your hand. Greyed-out cards can't be played right now. Hover over a card to see what it does.
    2. Click a parcel with a blue ring (or your depot, to bring one out).
    3. If there's a choice, click a yellow square to pick where it goes. With a 7, keep going until all 7 steps are used.
    4. Check the preview, then press Confirm (Enter), or Undo (Backspace) to choose again.

    With hidden hands and several human players, a blue screen covers the table between turns. Pass the laptop to the player it names. M turns the sound on or off.
